# 02 · Target segments

Four segments, ranked by how winnable they are and how much they're worth. Sizes are not estimated here because Apple and Spotify don't publish the data; the ranking is a judgement based on [01 · Market analysis](01-market-analysis.md).

| Rank | Segment | Why they're on Spotify | What would move them | Winnability |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **iPhone Spotify loyalists** | Library, playlists, friends, Wrapped | Bring everything across in one tap; a reason to try that Spotify can't match | High |
| 2 | **Graduating students** | Spotify Student was the default on campus | A better deal at the exact moment Spotify's student discount ends | High |
| 3 | **Shared households** | One person pays for Spotify Family | A household offer through Apple One | Medium |
| 4 | **Android listeners** | Spotify is the default habit on Android | Better Apple Music for Android, bundled with devices people already pay for | Low today, the long-term bet |

## 1 · iPhone Spotify loyalists (primary)

- **Who:** 18–34, iPhone and AirPods, has paid for Spotify Premium for years.
- **The real barrier:** *"All my music is on Spotify."* Ten years of playlists, liked songs and an algorithm that knows them.
- **The fear:** losing their Wrapped, their Blends with friends, and their Discover Weekly.
- **Opportunity:** they're already inside Apple's world. Apple Music is pre-installed, and AirPods, Apple Watch and CarPlay work best with it.

## 2 · Graduating students (the switch moment)

- **Who:** Spotify Student subscribers in their final year.
- **The moment:** when student eligibility ends, the Spotify bill roughly doubles (see the companion concept, [Spotify "Graduation Day"](https://github.com/Allsar2k/spotify-growth-concept)). They are, for one month, actively deciding.
- **Opportunity:** a graduate offer that lands in the same weeks, with Apple Music Student's Apple TV+ perk as a reason to have tried Apple first.

## 3 · Shared households

- **Who:** couples, roommates and families where one person pays for Spotify Family or Duo.
- **Opportunity:** Apple One Family bundles music with storage, TV and more, and the person who pays for the household's iCloud is often the same person who pays for music.

## 4 · Android listeners

- **Who:** the majority of the world's smartphone users.
- **Why rank them last now:** the switching cost is highest and Apple's advantages (AirPods, CarPlay, Watch) mostly don't apply. Growth here is real but slow; it's a product and partnership investment, not a campaign.

## Who this strategy doesn't target

Podcast-first and audiobook-first listeners. Spotify has invested heavily in both; competing there would cost a lot and doesn't play to Apple Music's strengths.
